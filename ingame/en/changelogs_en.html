Changelog - Star Grow

Here you can find all the updates and improvements made to Star Grow. Stay up to date with the latest news and changes!

Version 0.1.2.6 - September 1, 2026

  • Improved Purchase System: The purchase system has been updated to provide greater security, reliability, and compatibility.
  • Purchase Recovery: Improvements have been made to the detection and recovery of previously completed purchases.
  • Random Planet Names: Newly created planets now automatically receive a unique name and can still be renamed at any time.
  • Startup Improvements: The game startup process has been improved to reduce unexpected messages and provide greater stability.
  • Improved Message System: Game notifications and messages have been reorganized to appear more clearly and at the appropriate time.
  • Resource Conversion Fix: Fixed an issue that could generate incorrect values when converting Infinity Coupons into Creation Points.
  • General stability and compatibility improvements, along with various bug fixes.

Version 0.1.2.2 - January 14, 2025

  • New Progressive Missions: 8 more missions to help with your progression.
  • Ad Levels: Increase the rewards earned by watching ads. The higher the level, the greater the rewards.
  • Improvements and bug fixes.

Version 0.1.2.1 - January 12, 2025

  • New Progressive Missions: 22 new progressive missions to help with your progression.
  • More Asteroids: Maximum number of asteroids increased from 20 to 25.
  • New Creation Item, Massive Asteroids: Increases the amount of mass provided by asteroids.
  • New Creation Item, Impacting Asteroids: Increases the Impact Bonus provided by asteroids.
  • New Creation Item, Rest Time: Increases the energy accumulated through Offline Income.
  • Improvements and bug fixes.

Version 0.1.2.0 - December 26, 2024

  • New Menu, Daily Missions: Receive new missions every day. Each completed mission rewards you with Infinity Coupons.
  • Conversion Button: Convert Infinity Coupons into Creation Points in the Creation menu.
  • New Creation Item, Energetic Tap: Generate Energy whenever you tap the star.
  • New Creation Item, Tap Intensity: Increase the amount of Energy generated with each tap on the star.
  • Improvements and bug fixes.

Version 0.1.1.40 - December 11, 2024

  • Changes to the advertising system.
  • Translation of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  • Improvements and bug fixes.

Version 0.1.1.37 - November 13, 2024

  • Fixed behavior-related bugs.
  • Improved the game initialization system.
  • Added the News & Social menu (message balloon).

Version 0.1.0.86 - October 8, 2024

  • Initial release of Star Grow!
  • Basic star management features.
